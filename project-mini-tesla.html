Personal Project · April 2026 – Present

Mini Tesla — regenerative electric vehicle prototype.

In Progress Demo · Coming Soon

evdc motorscustom gearbox regenerative brakingpower distribution

A hands-on electric vehicle prototype designed to explore the core engineering ideas behind modern EVs: electric propulsion, drivetrain gearing, regenerative braking, and power distribution.

The project focuses on building a small-scale car platform that uses DC motors for motion while experimenting with a custom gearbox to improve torque, speed control, and incline traversal.

A major goal is studying regenerative braking at a practical level — how motor back-EMF, gear ratios, and motor-driver limitations affect the ability to recover energy when the vehicle slows down.

Highlights

  • Milestone-based development: component selection, schematic planning, and parts sourcing
  • Mechanical assembly and drivetrain testing
  • Electrical integration and regenerative braking analysis
  • Combines robotics, embedded systems, mechanical design, and EV fundamentals into one platform
