Personal Project · April 2026 – Present

Mini Tesla — autonomous EV & intelligent robotics platform.

Mini Tesla is a miniature electric vehicle I'm designing from scratch to explore advanced robotics, autonomous decision-making, predictive perception, and energy-efficient motion control.

The objective: a robot that doesn't just navigate its environment, but actively reasons about what it sees, predicts what happens next, and adapts its behavior in real time. Mini Tesla · the goal

Research & Development

Six goals, one platform.

Each goal builds on the last — from choosing where to look, to understanding what's there, to predicting where it's going and braking efficiently when it matters.

01

Active SLAM & Autonomous Exploration

Develop an Active SLAM system in ROS 2 that enables the robot to intelligently select where to explore next, reducing map uncertainty while balancing information gain, navigation safety, and computational cost.

02

Semantic SLAM & Environment Understanding

Go beyond traditional geometric mapping by integrating semantic object recognition into 3D maps. Using an iPhone Pro's camera and LiDAR, alongside additional onboard sensors, the goal is to identify, classify, and spatially track objects rather than represent the environment only as geometric obstacles.

03

Predictive Perception & Dynamic Object Tracking

Develop a perception pipeline capable of identifying moving objects, estimating their velocity and direction, and predicting future trajectories.

Demonstration goal Detect a football thrown or kicked toward the robot, estimate its trajectory, predict the potential collision point, and autonomously execute a braking or evasive maneuver before impact.
04

iPhone Pro as a Visual-LiDAR Perception System

Investigate using an iPhone Pro as an onboard RGB-D sensing platform, streaming camera and LiDAR-derived depth data into a ROS 2 environment running on Linux.

This involves bridging Apple's ARKit-based sensing system with the robot's localization, mapping, and perception pipelines, including investigating calibration, synchronization, and real-time streaming latency.

05

Predictive Motion Planning & Collision Avoidance

Develop a motion-planning system that responds to predicted future obstacle positions, not just their current locations. Explore model predictive control (MPC), time-to-collision estimation, and dynamic trajectory replanning to support safe, high-speed navigation.

06

Intelligent Regenerative Braking

Investigate energy-aware control strategies that combine autonomous motion planning with regenerative braking. The goal is to experimentally characterize energy recovery and evaluate whether predicted braking events can improve stopping performance and energy efficiency.

Mechanical Development

Designed and printed by me.

Designed entirely by me in Autodesk Fusion 360. The chassis uses four encoder-equipped geared DC motors with mecanum wheels, a modular mechanical architecture, and a dedicated platform for onboard computing and sensors.

All custom chassis components are fabricated using PLA on a Prusa MK4S 3D printer.

Chassis evolution

Version 1

Initial Architecture

Established the fundamental chassis layout, drivetrain positioning, and mechanical packaging requirements.

Version 2

Structural & Assembly Refinement

Focused on improving motor mounting, component clearances, structural support, and assembly accessibility.

Version 3 · Latest

Modular Robotics Platform

Refined the design for sensor integration, removable electronics, cable management, M2.5 fasteners, heat-set inserts, and mounting space for a laptop-based computing system.

Each iteration is driven by the practical challenges of turning a mechanical CAD model into a functional autonomous robotic vehicle.
