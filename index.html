About

I'm usually the one figuring out why the robot thinks it's two feet to the left.

I'm a Computer Engineering student at Virginia Tech (Class of 2028), specializing in robotics and autonomous systems. The messier the integration between hardware and software, the more I enjoy it.

I'm a software engineer on the Competitive Robotics Organization (CRO) team at Virginia Tech — where most of my real engineering education has happened, outside the classroom.

Right now I'm building a ROS 2 Warehouse Fleet Simulation and Mini Tesla, a regenerative EV prototype. Before them: Lunar ResQ and Mining Mayhem, competition robots involving ROS/ROS 2 autonomy, navigation, simulation, and hardware integration. When I'm not writing firmware, I'm running my Prusa MK4S, doing PCB work, or out shooting photos.
