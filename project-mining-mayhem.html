SoutheastCon Hardware Competition 2025 · September 2024 – March 2025

Mining Mayhem — autonomous object sorting robot.

rosslamapriltags wheel odometrydifferential drive

Engineered a ROS-based differential-drive robot with SLAM and real-time object-sorting capabilities for the SoutheastCon 2025 hardware competition.

The robot maps its arena, locates scattered objects, and sorts them autonomously — combining perception, localization, and manipulation in a single tightly-timed run.

Highlights

  • Utilized AprilTags and wheel odometry for localization and target detection, enabling fast mapping and precise alignment
  • Collaborated in a 3-member interdisciplinary team integrating hardware, simulation, and embedded software layers
