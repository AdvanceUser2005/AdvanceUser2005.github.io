Startup · 2023 – 2025

Plant-based automated system, designed and built end to end.

fusion 360pcb designatmega328p embedded cjst connectors

An automated system for plant care, designed and built end-to-end as part of an early-stage startup — from mechanical parts to a custom PCB to the embedded firmware running it all.

Every layer of the product was mine: the enclosure geometry, the board layout, the wiring harness, and the code on the microcontroller.

Highlights

  • Designed all mechanical parts and the enclosure in Fusion 360
  • Designed and fabricated a custom double-layer PCB, wired with JST connectors
  • Built a standalone microcontroller circuit around a bare ATmega328P at 16 MHz — the same core as an Arduino Uno, without the board
  • Assembled the electronics and enclosure into one finished device
